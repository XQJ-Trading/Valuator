### Pre-Escalation Context (October 2024)
In **October 2024**, following Israel's killing of Hamas and Hezbollah leaders, **Iran launched 180 ballistic missiles at Israel**. Israel responded with its largest direct attack on Iran, targeting air defenses and missile production facilities. This marked a shift from proxy conflicts to direct strikes, weakening Iran's "axis of resistance" amid the fall of Syria's Assad regime.[2]

### 2025 Developments Leading to 2026 War
A **12-day Israel-Iran war occurred in June 2025**, where Israel damaged Iran's air defenses, military, and nuclear sites. The U.S. struck underground nuclear facilities at Fordow, Natanz, and Isfahan, ending the conflict.[5]

### Major Escalation: 2026 Iran War (February 28 Onward)
The full-scale **U.S.-Israel war against Iran** began on **February 28, 2026**, under **Operation Epic Fury**. Key events:

- **February 27-28: Initial Strikes**  
  At 06:35 UTC on February 28, CENTCOM and partners launched airstrikes using Tomahawk missiles, HIMARS, B-2 stealth bombers, B-1 Lancers, and B-52s against ballistic missile sites, command centers, and Tehran targets. Strikes killed **Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei** and dozens of officials, including IRGC head Mohammad Pakpour. Nearly 900 strikes hit missiles, air defenses, infrastructure, and leadership in 12 hours; ~2,000 deaths reported across Iran, Lebanon, and Israel. U.S. President Trump announced operations to topple the regime, prevent nuclear/missile programs, neutralize naval forces, and protect interests. IDF targeted hundreds of sites, including missile launchers.[1][4][5]

- **Iran's Retaliation (February 28-March)**  
  Iran fired ~35 Emad and Ghadr missiles at Israel and struck 14 U.S. bases in Bahrain, UAE, Kuwait, Jordan, and Saudi Arabia. IRGC vowed continued attacks until U.S./Israel defeat.[1][4]

- **March 3: Expansion**  
  War involved nine countries. Joint U.S.-Israeli strikes hit Tehran’s Azadi Square, metro, military complex, Basij HQ, missile pads, and eastern outpost housing all Iranian security branches.[1]

- **March 4**  
  Seven more Iranian officials killed by Israel.[3]

- **March 6: Intensified Airstrikes**  
  U.S.-Israel conducted heavy strikes on Tehran (Mehrabad airport, police, IRGC base, residential units), Kermanshah, Salmas, Maku, and Saqqez; HRANA reported 664 attacks, 25 casualties (4 civilian deaths).[1]

### Ongoing Status (as of March 2026)
The war remains active, with U.S. operations ahead of the initial 4-6 week timeline. Objectives include regime change, nuclear dismantlement, and Axis of Resistance neutralization; Iran continues retaliatory barrages.[1][4][6]
