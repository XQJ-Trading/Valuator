### Pelican Satellite Launch Schedule
- **Recent activity (pre-2026):** Pelican-3 and -4 shipped to launch sites with production fully ramped; Pelican-5/-6 launched November 28, 2025, on SpaceX Falcon 9 Transporter-15, delivering first-light images by December 4, 2025; earlier Pelican-1 (Nov 2023) and Pelican-2 (Jan 2025).[1][3][4]
- **2026 outlook:** Gen-2 Pelicans (30 cm resolution, 6 km swath, multiple daily revisits) start launching, building to a 30-satellite constellation (including 10 prioritized for Japan’s SKY Perfect JSAT under a $230M deal); "a few more" launches expected soon after recent ones, with full interoperability across Planet's fleet.[1][2][5][6][7]
- Gen-1 specs: 40-50 cm resolution, 6 multispectral bands, 8 km swath, NVIDIA AI chips for on-orbit processing.[1][3][7]

### Tanager Satellite Status

- 2026 role: Key to advanced Earth observation alongside Pelicans, with hyperspectral capabilities enhancing Planet's data stack.[6]
