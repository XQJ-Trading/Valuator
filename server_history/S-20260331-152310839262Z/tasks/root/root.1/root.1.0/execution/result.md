Planet Labs PBC (PL) derives a growing portion of revenue from government customers (including defense/intelligence and civil government), with **defense & intelligence revenue up over 50% year-over-year in fiscal 2026**, while **commercial revenue declined modestly year-over-year** amid a strategic shift toward government contracts and agriculture headwinds.[4][2] Civil government revenue was **flat year-over-year in fiscal 2026**, impacted by the end of the Norway NICFI contract.[4]

### Revenue Mix Details (Fiscal Year 2026, Ended January 31, 2026)
- Total revenue reached **$307.7 million**, up 26% year-over-year, driven primarily by defense/intelligence growth exceeding 50%.[2][4][6]
- **Government overall**: Accelerating, with Q4 FY2026 revenue growth of 41% year-over-year; includes major deals like a €240 million German government contract, U.S. DoD DIU expansions, NRO, and NATO surveillance.[1][2]
- **Commercial**: Down modestly year-over-year but showed 6% YoY growth in Q2 FY2026 (ended July 31, 2025) and 13% QoQ, led by agriculture (e.g., FarmDar) and energy; represents a reacceleration after prior declines.[1][4]
- Recurring annual contract value (ACV) comprised **98% of total ACV** at FY2026 year-end, underscoring subscription reliance.[2]

### Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO) and Backlog
- **RPO** climbed **106% year-over-year to $852 million** at FY2026 end (January 31, 2026).[2]
- **Total backlog** grew **79% year-over-year to over $900 million** (some reports cite exactly $900 million), reflecting expanded government/defense contracts and satellite services like the $230 million SKY Perfect JSAT deal (revenue recognized over ~7 years starting 2027).[1][2][3]
- Earlier Q4 FY2025 (ended January 31, 2025): RPO at **$407.5 million (+179% QoQ)**; backlog at **$498.5 million (+115% QoQ)**.[3]

### Debt Ratio and Cash Flow Health
Search results from the latest filings (FY2026 10-K/10-Q equivalents via press releases and transcripts) lack specific debt ratios (e.g., debt-to-equity or net debt figures) or detailed balance sheet breakdowns like total debt outstanding.[2][3][4][6] GAAP results showed losses due to warrant accounting charges, but adjusted metrics improved sharply.[2]
- **Cash flow**: Achieved **first full year of adjusted EBITDA profitability ($15.5 million)** and **free cash flow profitability** in FY2026, versus a $10.6 million adjusted EBITDA loss in FY2025; Q4 adjusted EBITDA was $2.3 million.[2]
- Non-GAAP gross margin: **59%** for FY2026 (down slightly from 60% in FY2025).[4]
